# APTET Quiz App - A Journey of Building

## What is This Project?

This is a quiz application built to help my father, Prasad, prepare for the **APTET (Andhra Pradesh Teachers Eligibility Test)**. As his son, I created this application from scratch to provide him with a structured, interactive way to practice and track his progress.

## The Story: How This Project Grew

This project didn't start perfect. It grew step by step, solving one problem at a time. Here's the honest journey:

### Stage 1: The First Quiz (June 9, 2026)
**The Problem:** My father needed a way to practice online, but there was nothing available.

**What I Built:** I created the very first quiz interface with basic HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It was simple - just questions and answers on a screen. Day 1 was ready, and we had our foundation.

### Stage 2: Making It Better (June 9-10, 2026)
**The Problem:** The quiz looked ugly and didn't show where he was going in the test. The explanations were in the middle of the screen, making it hard to read them while answering.

**What I Fixed:**
- Added a "Goal" display (120+ marks target) to keep him motivated
- Fixed the explanation box to stay at the bottom - much easier to read
- Added a "Next Question" button for smooth navigation
- Changed how the app loads so it works properly when deployed
- Added subject pills/badges so you can see what topic each question covers

### Stage 3: The Smart Options Lock Feature (June 14, 2026)
**The Problem:** My father was rushing through questions without reading them properly. He would just glance and try to guess the answer.

**What I Added:** A timer that locks the answer options for 15 seconds. This forces him to read the question carefully before he can even click an answer. No rushing, no guessing.

**Later Update (July 2026):** Reduced the lock timer from 15 seconds to 10 seconds for a better balance between thinking time and test-taking speed.

### Stage 4: Building 30 Days of Practice (June 14 - July 10, 2026)
**The Problem:** One day of questions isn't enough. He needs continuous practice.

**What I Did:** I systematically added:
- Day 1 through Day 30 - 30 complete days of quiz questions
- Regular daily quizzes to build consistency
- Mock tests every week (Days 6, 13, 20, etc.) to simulate real exam pressure
- Different subjects covered across all 30 days

Each day built on the previous one. The app grew stronger with more content.

### Stage 5: The Smart Report Feature (July 12, 2026)
**The Problem:** He completes a quiz, gets a score, but doesn't know what mistakes he made or what topics need more work.

**What I Added:** A "Mistakes Report" that generates at the end of every quiz. Now when he finishes:
- He sees his score with a visual progress circle
- Gets detailed statistics (correct answers, wrong answers)
- Can download a PDF report to review later
- Knows exactly which questions he got wrong and why

This helps him identify weak areas and focus his preparation.

## How to Use It

1. Open the app and see the personalized greeting
2. Check how many days are left until APTET
3. Start a quiz - you'll see the question count and your progress bar
4. Read the question (options are locked for 10 seconds, so no rushing)
5. After 10 seconds, select your answer
6. Read the explanation at the bottom to understand why that's the right answer
7. Click "Next Question" to continue
8. After finishing all questions, see your score report
9. Download the PDF report to track your mistakes

## Why I Built This

1. Is tailored specifically for him
2. Tracks his real mistakes

So I started with one day of quizzes and kept improving it based on what he needed.
